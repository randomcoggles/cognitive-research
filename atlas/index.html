How to use Release 1

Reference, application, and assessment

1 · Learn the distinctions

Each domain introduces capabilities, learning objectives, workplace examples, and common failure modes.

2 · Build your notebook

Add comments, questions, tasks, or custom notes to every domain section, then review them in your learning dashboard.

3 · Test application

Complete each seven-question assessment, inspect your progress, and transfer notes and results between devices with a portable export.

Incremental product roadmap

What this release deliberately does not pretend to finish

Now · Domain foundation

Index, search, shared taxonomy, twelve substantive domains, concept pages, knowledge graph, learning paths, cognitive profile, Human ↔ Tool ↔ AI mappings, local progress, and interactive assessment.

Next · Concept depth

Richer concept pages, relationship-mapping and reflection questions, and content for the remaining learning-path steps.

Later · Adaptive learning

Adaptive quizzes, spaced review, and an AI tutor.

Release 1 is a standalone local prototype based on the Atlas PRD. Notes and progress are stored only in this browser unless you export them. Assessment scores support reflection; they are not psychological measurements.
